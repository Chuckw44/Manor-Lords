Best Export Rankings

Net Trade Value compares what an item sells for with the value of the materials used to make it. Raw resources use their sale price directly. Use the strategy guidance alongside the ranking because labor, fuel, town demand, and Trade Route access can change which export is best at a given stage. Pelts and Wax have no other current use, making excess stock good to sell even though they are not always included in the Best Exports ranking.

Notes & Assumptions

  • Recommendations assume typical long-term gameplay.

  • Origin recommendations focus on effects that materially impact production or trade.
  • Recipes and mechanics are based on the current beta game version 8.097.
