Fish reproduce only on March 1, April 1 and May 1. Each spawn adds 40% of the current stock, rounded up, up to the node's cap. The ideal reserve is cap ÷ 1.4 (about 71.4%), which maximizes each spring spawn.

Seasonal assignment option: After the May 1 spawn, full staffing is no longer necessary. You can unassign the spring workforce and use 1–2 families in a later season to finish the remaining harvest gradually. This helps avoid catching a large surplus at once that will spoil before your peasants can consume it.
