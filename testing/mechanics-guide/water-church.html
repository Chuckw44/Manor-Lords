Water & Church

Water and church access are straightforward burgage requirements. Wells provide water to a fixed number of plots, while the church requirement advances as burgage plots reach higher levels.

Church Requirements

The required church tier rises with burgage level. A higher-tier church also satisfies the requirements of lower-tier plots.
