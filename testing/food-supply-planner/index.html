Reference units follow the collected production charts: field crops are shown per 1 Morgen (350 plants). Vegetable and orchard chart values are converted to a +50% Burgage Plot: approximately 322 vegetable plants or 32 orchard trees. Vegetable +50% plot baselines are normalized from tested annual production measured at 1.10× efficiency: Beets 56, Carrots 62, Cabbage 85 food/year at 1.10×. These are normalized to 1.00× (Beets 50.91, Carrots 56.36, Cabbage 77.27), then multiplied by the current Pollination efficiency. Crop, Orchard, and Vegetable yields use the in-game Pollination rule: Efficiency = 1 + 0.30 × (Meadow + Apiation), with Apiation adding 2% per Apiary up to a 10% cap. For orchard fruit, Cow-equivalent means how many cows would produce the same raw annual Milk output at your current Meadow percentage. In Top Choices, “Used” is now a calculated quantity. The current baseline divides annual town food demand evenly across the number of distinct food sources required by the settlement, then calculates how many backyard plots, Morgens, or cows would be needed for each selected source. For cattle, the displayed unit is the breeding herd. Milk output is shown per breeding cow in Fruit/Animal Produce, while sustainable Beef is shown per breeding cow in Meats/Fishes from expected calf production. The breeding cows are not being counted as slaughtered animals.
